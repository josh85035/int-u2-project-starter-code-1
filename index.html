Fight Food Insecurity

How to fight against it?

To fight against food Insecurity, you can...

  • Support charities and movements
  • Promote Education
  • Encourage sustainable food practices

What is it

Food Insecurity is...

when families, households, or people, do not have reliable acesss to food, especially safe and nutritious food.

Causes

Food insecurity is caused by...

  • Economic constraints, such as low-income or unemployment.
  • Access barriors, such as phsyically lacking transportation or systemically lacking access
  • Global factors, such as conflicts, wars, climate change, and supply chain disruptions
